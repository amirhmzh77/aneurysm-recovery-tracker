Last 14 days

Call 999 now for

  • Sudden severe "worst" headache, or headache that keeps getting worse
  • Repeated vomiting, or growing drowsiness / hard to wake
  • New weakness, numbness, face drooping, slurred speech, confusion
  • Seizure or fainting
  • A new large pupil, or sudden vision loss
Call 999

Call neurosurgery the same day for

  • Fever 38°C or above, or chills
  • Redness, swelling, warmth or fluid at the scalp wound, shunt path (behind ear / neck) or belly
  • Headache worse day by day, or old pre-shunt symptoms returning
  • Belly pain or bloating
  • Eyelid droop clearly worse, other lid starting to droop, or new double vision
Call clinic

Good to know

Droopy right eyelid: doctor expects 3 to 6 months to recover. Protect the eye, use drops if advised, take extra care on stairs. Do not drive until the doctor clears it.
